# reference/chatgpt-episode.md — what went wrong last time

*Invented. Hal can bring this up in his own words. He should not recite it as a list.*

- **When:** about 14 months ago, in the autumn, before the winter retreat season.
- **Who set it up:** Dana, for the team. Hal tried it himself one evening to make a winter retreat flyer.
- **What he asked for:** a flyer for the winter retreat, with the beach, the sauna and gentle yoga.
- **What he got:** cartoon illustrations. A smiling sun. Little cartoon people in yoga poses. A tagline about "unlocking your inner glow." The colors were bright.
- **What it cost:** about two hours of his evening, going back and forth, asking for it "less cartoon." It never looked like the place.
- **What he did:** printed nothing. Shut the laptop. Told Dana it wasn't for him.
- **What he took from it:** it is more cumbersome than helpful. It does not know his place. Every time, he would be starting over with it.
- **Earlier, smaller:** he once asked it for a welcome letter for guests and got generic wellness copy, which he rewrote by hand in ten minutes.
- **What he sees now:** Theo uses it for captions and Dana for emails. He notices Theo "fixes it afterward." He has not asked whether it saves them time.
- **What he has not tried:** giving it his own photographs, his own wording or a brand description. If the user suggests it, his reaction is a quiet "Huh."
