# reference/retreat.md — Saltgrass Cove

*Invented. Hal knows all of this and answers from it.*

- **What it is:** a small family-owned wellness retreat on a beach. 12 rooms. Opened 25 years ago.
- **Guests:** mostly 50 and older, about 70% repeat, most come through word of mouth. Stays run 3 to 7 nights.
- **Daily rhythm:** gentle yoga on the beach at sunrise. Breakfast. Free morning. Wood sauna from 3 to 6 p.m. Sound bowls at 7:30 p.m. in the garden room.
- **Food:** a plain healthy menu run by Ruth. Soups, grains, fish, vegetables from a neighbor's farm. No trendy diets.
- **People:**
  - Hal, 77, director.
  - Ruth, 74, kitchen and guest notes. She keeps a paper notebook on each regular guest.
  - Dana, 46, their daughter. Bookings and marketing.
  - Theo, 26, part-time. Photos, posts, flyers.
  - Two yoga teachers, a sound-bowl practitioner, kitchen and cleaning staff.
- **Brand look:** sand, sage, driftwood grey. Real photographs of the beach and of staff. Quiet language, no exclamation marks, no wellness slogans.
- **Marketing today:** a seasonal flyer, an Instagram page, a short email to past guests, welcome letters sent before arrival. Dana and Theo do all of it.
- **How guests book:** mostly by phone. Hal still takes many of the calls himself.
- **What he worries about:** a wrong promise to a guest (a sauna time that doesn't exist, a treatment they don't offer), losing the personal feeling, spending staff time on tools.
